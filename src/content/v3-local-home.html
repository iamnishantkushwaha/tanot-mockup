FAQs

What digital marketing services does Tanot offer?
We run SEO and AI search, link building, paid media (Google and Meta ads), content marketing, social media, and website and conversion work. Each channel has its own specialist, and you have one account lead.
Do you work with businesses in India and abroad?
Yes. We work with 150+ brands across countries such as the US, UK, Australia and Bulgaria, as well as businesses in India.
How soon will I see results from SEO, ads and social?
Paid ads can start producing leads within weeks. SEO usually shows early improvements within 3 to 6 months, with stronger, compounding growth over 6 to 12 months. Social builds demand over time. We never promise overnight rankings; you get a realistic roadmap and regular progress updates.
How does performance-based pricing work?
There is no upfront payment. You pay for verified deliverables at the end of the month, and for link building you pay only for links that are live.
What budget do I need to get started?
Pricing starts at $500 a month and is then customized based on your goals, channels and requirements.
Is your link building safe and Google-friendly?
Yes. We never use private blog networks, bulk link packages or spammy directories. Every link is earned through genuine outreach on relevant sites with real audiences, and you approve the sites before anything goes live.
How do you help my brand appear in AI search (ChatGPT, AI Overviews)?
We optimise your content, structure and authority so AI tools mention and cite your brand, and we track your visibility in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews alongside your Google rankings.
How and how often will I get reports?
You get a live dashboard you can open any time, plus a monthly review call that walks through leads, sales and ROI, not just rankings and impressions.
Can I hire you for one service only?
Yes. You can start with a single service such as SEO, link building or paid media and add others when you are ready.
Do you offer white-label services for agencies?
Yes. We partner with agencies worldwide to deliver work under their brand, with unbranded reports and complete confidentiality.
